Identity findings

Publishing a code with "conferred by" requires knowing which company assigned it, and finding that company in the trial registry and on Wikidata. These findings are recorded, not resolved: who "the company" is across subsidiaries, joint ventures, mergers and renames is a general question. Source file: wikidata/company_identity_findings.md.

Successor sponsors

The registry holds the record as it is today. ONC201 (Oncoceutics) is in Jazz Pharmaceuticals' trials, NT-814 (NeRRe) in Bayer's, CNSA-001 (Censa) in PTC Therapeutics'. Dapagliflozin's BMS-512148 trials from 2005 have AstraZeneca as lead sponsor; whether the records were transferred is not visible.

Company items on Wikidata

Several smaller companies exist only as items labelled "<name> (<country>)", described as "company in <city>", apparently imported from an organisation register. Some companies have no item (Milestone Pharmaceuticals, Censa Pharmaceuticals, AnHeart Therapeutics). One item carries another company's current label: MedImmune is now labelled "AstraZeneca (United States)", so a "conferred by" pointing to it displays the later name.

Codes for programmes, not substances

Novo Nordisk's NN codes for semaglutide each follow one formulation × indication programme: NN9535 subcutaneous for type 2 diabetes, NN9924 tablets, NN9536 obesity. The compound code is NNC0113-0217. The level sits between active moiety and product. Neither Wikidata nor ISO IDMP has it.
